ABOUT SKY AIRWAYS

Fly Beyond Limits

Sky Airways is a student flight-reservation demonstration designed to make searching for a journey, choosing a seat, and managing a booking simple.

✈ Flexible routesSearch between any two different city names.
💺 Seat selectionChoose from available seats for each simulated flight.
🎫 Booking managementView your reservation details or cancel a booking.
This is a demonstration project. It does not connect to real airline schedules, payments, or ticket inventory.
